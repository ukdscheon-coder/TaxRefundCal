Data & Privacy (UK GDPR)

All records are stored locally on this device only in accordance with the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018. No pupil data is transmitted to external servers.

About WeNote UK

WeNote UK is a school counselling journal for teachers and counsellors working in UK schools. All data is stored locally on this device — no accounts, no cloud, no internet required.
